## Description

# PandaQuest Auction

See what items are worth without leaving the auction house. PandaQuest Auction adds a **PQ** tab with a full scan, an option to search for missing prices and progress tracking as results arrive.

### Install

**Requires PandaQuest**, which uses **PandaQuest DB**. Install all three addons for the same game client and restart WoW. PandaQuest Auction is optional for normal questing.

Supports **Mists of Pandaria Classic, Classic Era, The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary and WoW Forever**. Auction details such as quantities and seller counts depend on the game client.

### What you can do

- Run a native auction scan or make targeted searches for items that need recent prices. The native scanner works without another auction addon.
- Look up a price with `/pq price <item name, ID, or link>` when Auctionator or TradeSkillMaster has a quote. Shift-click the item into chat to use its link.
- Optionally share supported completed Auctionator scans.
- Optionally record your own listings and completed sales privately. This setting is separate from public price sharing.

### Prices and history on PandaBase

The addon saves observations locally when you log out or reload. The separate [PandaQuest Sync app](https://pd.zroot.it/setup) can upload those observations to [PandaBase](https://pd.zroot.it/). Use PandaBase's Auction prices pages to browse shared prices or view your own auction history after signing in.
